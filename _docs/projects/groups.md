When you have a lot of projects, groups (folders) keep the dashboard tidy. A group
is a labeled folder on the dashboard, in the spirit of app folders on a phone home
screen: open it to see the projects inside, collapse it to keep the grid short.

## Creating and filling groups

- **Create a group** and give it a name.
- **Add projects to a group** by dragging a card onto the folder, from the group's
  own view, or from the bulk bar.
- **Move a project** between groups the same way; drag it out to ungroup it.

Projects that aren't in any group stay loose on the dashboard alongside the folders.

## Arranging

- **Drag to reorder** — arrange both groups and loose projects in the order you
  want; the layout is saved and persists across restarts.
- **Filter** — the dashboard filter narrows what's shown; groups still frame the
  results.

## Bulk actions

Select multiple projects to act on them at once:

- **Select all** from the bulk bar to grab everything currently shown.
- Move the selection into a group, or apply a bulk action, in one step.

## Everything stays local

Groups are just a way to arrange your own dashboard — like the rest of Granspace's
data, they live in the local database on your machine. See
[Organizing]({{ '/docs/projects/organizing/' | relative_url }}) for stage, purpose,
tags and favorites.
