Found a bug, want a feature, or have an idea to improve Granspace? Open an issue on
the **feedback repository**:

<p><a class="btn" href="{{ site.repos.feedback }}/issues/new">Open an issue →</a></p>

That's the place for bug reports, feature requests and suggestions. Browse the
[existing issues]({{ site.repos.feedback }}/issues) first — someone may have already
raised it, and you can add your thoughts or a 👍 instead of opening a duplicate.

## A good report includes

- **What happened** and **what you expected**.
- **Steps to reproduce** it.
- Your **OS** and the **Granspace version** (Help → About).
- A screenshot or the relevant log, if it helps.

Thanks for helping make Granspace better.
